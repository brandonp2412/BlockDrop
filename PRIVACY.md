# BlockDrop Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 October 2026

BlockDrop is a free and open-source falling-block puzzle game.

## Data collection

BlockDrop does not sell personal data or use it for advertising or analytics. The app has no developer-operated account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or in-app purchases.

## Data stored on your device

BlockDrop stores game preferences, settings, and gameplay information such as high scores locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app functionality and is not sent to the developer.

## Multiplayer

BlockDrop includes optional local-network multiplayer. On a local network, the app can discover and communicate directly with another BlockDrop device on the same local network. Gameplay/session messages are exchanged directly between the participating devices and are not routed through or retained by developer-operated servers.

When you use online multiplayer in a browser, a Cloudflare Worker exchanges a temporary room key and WebRTC connection details, which can include IP addresses. The service uses those details to connect the players. The encrypted connection carries gameplay and scores between them. It does not send game state to the signalling service.

## Network access and external links

The app uses network access for multiplayer and may open external links, such as the BlockDrop source repository or sponsor page, in your browser. Any external site you choose to visit is governed by that site's own privacy practices.

## Children

BlockDrop is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If BlockDrop's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released where required.
