Drop blocks, clear lines, and beat your high score in a classic falling-block puzzle.

BlockDrop is a fast, focused arcade puzzle game built around the familiar challenge of placing falling tetrominoes. Rotate and move each piece, complete horizontal lines, and keep the board clear as the pace rises.

PLAY YOUR WAY

• Use all seven classic tetromino pieces
• Move, rotate, soft drop, and hard drop with responsive controls
• Hold a piece for the right moment
• Plan ahead with the next-piece preview
• Track score and line clears as the game speeds up
• Choose light, dark, or AMOLED-friendly themes

MADE FOR QUICK PUZZLE SESSIONS

Whether you have a few minutes or want to chase a new personal best, BlockDrop gives you a clean block-stacking game without unnecessary distractions. It works offline and is available across mobile and desktop platforms.

BlockDrop is free and open source. It contains no ads, in-app purchases, tracking, or data collection.
