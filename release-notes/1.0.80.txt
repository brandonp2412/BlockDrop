Play a two-player match in your browser. Open Settings, choose Play online, and send your opponent an invitation link. Installed apps still support LAN play.

Multiplayer shows one result per match. Boards and results are centered on wide screens. The opponent preview is off by default. Turn it on in Settings and long-press it to hide it again. Full-screen touch controls sit below the board.
